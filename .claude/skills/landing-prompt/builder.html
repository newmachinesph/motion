landing-prompt · builder

Six gated stages, in the order a studio works. Each stands in for a role and must pass its gate before the next opens. Emits the numbered folder — brief, content matrix, direction, composition, DESIGN.md, and the buildable spec.

Discovery · strategist

Stage 0 — Brief

Answer these rather than asking them. A studio bills for discovery because the client cannot answer unprompted — so decide, and have it corrected.

Brief

The highest-value line and the most skipped. One sentence kills more bad directions than a page of positive description.

Content · content strategist

Stage 1 — Content matrix

Late content is the largest cause of slipped launches, and designing against lorem ipsum is why layouts break when real words arrive at twice the length. Write the copy here and the spec carries it verbatim instead of the builder inventing it.

Art direction · art director

Stage 2 — Direction, and the sign-off

The last cheap moment to redirect — nothing after this stage is cheap to change. Pick one, and record why the two nearest alternatives were declined. A direction nobody rejected anything for is a direction nobody chose.

Chosen direction

A four-to-six word image naming a thing, not a feeling. It fixes ground, mood, accent and density in one line — and every later value defends it.

UX · information architect

Archetype fixes the language. The six axes stop two briefs in one archetype landing on the same page — answer each from the brief, not from preference.
